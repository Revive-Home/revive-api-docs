<Update label="September 25, 2026" description="admin-v3.17.2" tags={["admin"]}>

### New

- Listing photo galleries display photos in the order provided, excluding photos removed by the listing provider. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))
- Photo galleries show the latest selection when you open another listing before an earlier photo request finishes. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))
- Listing scores now reflect price-based adjustments for development opportunities and use a 90-point ceiling. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))
- Score explanations clarify how recommended products are selected and how their badges relate to listing scores. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))
- Added Sales Pipeline, Sell 360, Flip 360, and Renovate to Sell pages and calculators. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Added deal sheets for renovation budgets, property details, offer breakdowns, and selling options, with calculator results connected to deal information. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Added map pages for active construction sites and home seller referrals. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Property addresses can open in Google Maps, and comparable listings can show saved exterior photos with improved image fallbacks. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Weekly updates can generate in the background and provide concise status summaries. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))

### Improved

- Sales and Advisor tabs are ordered consistently across opportunity workflows. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Updated weekly update headings and formatting for easier reading. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- Improved comparable property details, document handling, and signing-session error messages. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))

### Fixed

- Requests for photos from a listing that cannot be found now return a clearer “Listing not found” message. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))
- Closing a photo gallery clears its photos and loading state, preventing results from an earlier request from reappearing. ([#533](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/533))

### Action required

No action required for existing integrations.

</Update>
