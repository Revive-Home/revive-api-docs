<Update label="December 23, 2025" description="v1.78.0" tags={["mobile"]}>

**Highlights:** The Marketing Center in the mobile app now shows a personalized Revive Wrapped video at the top of the list, which you can play and download.

### Mobile

**New**

- **Revive Wrapped video in the Marketing Center:** Your personalized Revive Wrapped video now appears at the top of the Marketing Center when one is available. Play a preview in the app and download it to share. ([mobile#503](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-mobile/pull/503))

**Improved**

- **Open the Marketing Center to a specific item:** The Marketing Center can now open directly to a specific item, which makes links into it more flexible. ([mobile#503](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-mobile/pull/503))
