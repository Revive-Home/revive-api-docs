**Improved**

- **Google Maps links and exterior photos on comparables:** Property addresses open in Google Maps. Comparable listings can show saved exterior photos, with better fallbacks when an image is missing. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- **Weekly updates generate in the background:** Weekly updates now generate in the background and include a short status summary. Headings and formatting are updated to make them easier to read. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))
- **Consistent Sales and Advisor tab order:** Sales and Advisor tabs appear in the same order across all opportunity workflows. ([#526](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/526))

- **Listing scores for development opportunities:** Listing scores now include price-based adjustments for development opportunities and top out at 90 points. **Fixed**
