<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="admin-v3.15.0, dashboard-v2.13.0, api-v3.12.0" tags={["dashboard","admin","api","platform"]}>

**Highlights:** The Dashboard gets a new construction project view, and Admin gets RMA bulk actions and partial invoice payments. A quota guard also stops "Update materials" from wiping agents' marketing kits.

### Dashboard

**New**

- **Construction project dashboard:** Follow a renovation in one place, with summary, log, scope, photos, assets, team, marketing, and contract views. Timelines, weekly updates, and check-ins keep everyone current. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))
- **Team invitations, comments, and notifications:** Invite team members, edit their roles, and leave comments and feedback on a project. Notification links now open the right project page. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))

**Improved**

- **Update materials pauses when image quota is low:** The "Update materials" button is disabled with a "try again later" message when the image service is nearly out of quota. This keeps an agent's kit from being deleted and left empty. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- **Lead-capture links use a new address:** Agent QR codes, copied links, and menu actions now use /reviveai/. Old /revive-ai/ links redirect, so printed QR codes and shared links keep working. ([#254](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/254))

**Fixed**

- One failed template no longer stops the rest of an agent's marketing kit from being rebuilt. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- Photos that can't be converted from HEIC now upload as the original file instead of failing. ([#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))
- Meetings with no organizer information no longer cause errors. ([#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))
- Uploaded images and logos with accents or special characters in the file name now save and download correctly. ([#372](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/372))

### Admin

**New**

- **Nearest contractors on opportunities:** Opportunity overviews now list the closest contractors with names, phone numbers, and distances, plus a map. Five show by default, and you can see up to 20. ([#414](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/414))
- **Bulk status updates on the RMA list:** Select several RMA items and mark them Paid, Hold, or Not Approved in one step, with a confirmation and notes. Locked or completed items can't be changed by accident. ([#410](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/410))
- **Partial payments on outside vendor invoices:** Outside vendor payments are grouped by invoice with progress and balances. Start a payment request from an invoice's remaining balance with details prefilled. Requests can't exceed the balance. ([#411](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/411))
- **MLS photo import in Revive AI intake:** Import photos from a listing URL straight into the intake uploads. If some photos fail to download, you get a warning. If none download, nothing is imported. ([#378](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/378))

**Improved**

- **Construction project management tools:** Staff can manage renovation activities, agreement statuses, required-document workflows, and project timelines for the new construction dashboard. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))
- **Marketing center bulk buttons pause when quota is low:** "Add to all agents" and "Add to one agent" are disabled with a tooltip when the image quota is low. "Remove from all agents" stays available. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- **RMA tabs, filters, and CSV export:** RMA tabs have their own columns, sorting, filters, and date ranges, and they remember your settings. You can export selected rows to CSV. Date filters and totals now respect time zones. ([#410](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/410))
- **RMA progress in finance views:** Finance views now show RMA progress, statuses, dates, and final indicators, including on smaller screens. ([#411](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/411))
- **Contract previews from the RMA list:** Preview a contract from the RMA list. Finalized contracts download, and drafts open for editing. RMA details now show contract ID, deal type, pipeline stage, and requested amount. ([#380](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/380))
- **Weekly update schedule checks:** If a project's end date has passed, publishing a weekly update now requires a future replacement date. Warnings and date guidance are clearer, and on-schedule updates no longer show a date range. ([#395](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/395))

**Fixed**

- Delayed-project details now stay accurate when weekly updates are approved, unpublished, or deleted. ([#395](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/395))

### API

**New**

- **AI review of equity documents:** Uploaded listing agreements, mortgage statements, title reports, trust documents, and reconveyances are reviewed automatically for missing, outdated, or unclear information. Admins can rerun reviews and get results in Slack. ([#398](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/398))
- **Active constructions map:** Properties in construction appear as pins on the Atlist map, updated nightly. Pins show deal stage, deal type, renovation amount, and a HubSpot link. They are removed at Check Received. ([#391](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/391), [#405](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/405))

**Fixed**

- Completed contract, estimate, and RMA PDFs from PandaDoc now download more reliably, with automatic retries and clearer errors. ([#370](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/370))
- Contracts that are already completed are no longer processed twice, and partial signer progress is handled correctly. ([#370](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/370))

### Platform

**Improved**

- **Firestore security rules locked down:** Security rules are now version controlled. Writes require sign-in, staging deploys automatically, and production is deployed manually. ([#377](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/377))
- **Preview checks and cleaner error reports:** Preview builds now point at the pull request's own review API, and a check fails if they don't. Error reports ignore more browser-extension noise. ([#394](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/394), [#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))

</Update>

<Update label="August 13, 2026" description="admin-v3.14.0, dashboard-v2.12.0, api-v3.11.0" tags={["dashboard","admin","platform"]}>

**Highlights:** Admin adds outside vendor payment requests, plus AI-written weekly update summaries and document uploads in weekly updates. The dashboard report header gets a cleaner More menu and a home photos button.

### Dashboard

**New**

- **Ad click tracking at signup:** Ad-platform click IDs, such as Google and Meta, are now captured during getting started and attached to the person's HubSpot contact. This keeps ad attribution for people who sign up in the app, without blocking onboarding. ([#343](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/343))

**Improved**

- **Cleaner report actions and a home photos button:** Customize, View, Activity and Delete now sit in one More menu for agents. Homeowners get a Share menu with Copy link and Download PDF. Both views have an Add home photos button that shows the photo count once photos exist. ([#341](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/341))

**Fixed**

- After you pick an address, a suggestion row no longer reappears and tempts a second click. A "Loading your property…" message shows while the next step loads. ([#347](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/347))
- Choosing "Yes, add additional photos" now shows a "Processing your photos…" message right away, instead of looking frozen for a long time on large photos. ([#359](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/359))
- One unreadable or corrupt photo no longer stalls the whole photo upload. JPG and PNG files also process faster. ([#359](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/359))
- Comparable photos in Revive AI reports show "Loading photos…" while they load, instead of flashing "No photos available" first. ([#346](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/346))
- Project timelines, scopes, progress reports and weekly updates now show the verified start and end dates when available, instead of the original dates. ([#355](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/355))
- Saving a profile no longer errors when the brokerage hasn't changed. Brokerage search also no longer requires a website to show a result. ([#290](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/290))

### Admin

**New**

- **Outside vendor payment requests:** Create payment requests for outside vendors with ACH or payment-link options, W-9 confirmation and notes. Each request has a detail page with status actions, activity history and rush-payment handling. You can also add new vendors. ([#271](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/271))
- **Contract breakdown on RMA pages:** RMA detail pages now show the contract breakdown: contract details, itemized amounts, payments collected and the remaining balance. It refreshes when key RMA details change. ([#365](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/365))
- **Documents in weekly updates:** Attach documents, such as PDFs, to a weekly update alongside photos. Previews show file details and PDF page counts. Unsupported files and files over 25 MB are rejected with a clear message. ([#333](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/333))
- **AI summaries for contractor weekly updates:** When a contractor submits a weekly update, an AI-written summary is generated in the background using available deal details. Submission isn't delayed, and you can regenerate the summary if needed. ([#362](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/362))
- **Subcontractor pipeline tab:** The Contractors section has a new Subcontractor tab, and subcontractors appear in contractor search. Their pages hide project stats and deal details and give more room to contact information. ([#360](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/360))
- **Cloud photo folder links on deals:** Link a Dropbox, Google Drive, Google Photos, OneDrive or iCloud folder to a deal. The link is checked before saving, shows a provider badge on the deal's photo thumbnail, and opens securely in a new tab. ([#354](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/354))
- **Changes highlighted in estimate version history:** Version history now marks added, edited, recalculated and removed estimate items, with subtotal differences and AI-written tooltips. Removed items stay visible as read-only. Each version shows the total estimate. ([#316](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/316))

**Improved**

- **Outside vendor payments and RMAs in Finance dashboards:** Finance dashboards now show outside vendor payments and RMAs together, with grouped check-run views and CSV exports. Payments and RMAs both support a rush payment date. ([#271](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/271))
- **Execution and rush payment dates on the RMA dashboard:** The RMA dashboard has sortable Execution Date and Rush Payment Date columns, and both dates are included in CSV exports. Missing dates show as "-". ([#334](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/334))
- **Weekly updates use the publish wizard:** Creating, previewing and publishing a weekly update now all go through the publish wizard. It opens in a new tab, and the list refreshes when you return. The older edit and create flows are gone. ([#363](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/363))
- **Notes in Slack payment and RMA alerts:** Slack notifications for payments and RMAs now include the submitted note when there is one. Special characters in notes no longer break the message formatting. ([#315](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/315))

**Fixed**

- Declining a rush payment or RMA rush request clears its old payment date, so a stale date no longer lingers. ([#334](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/334))
- A weekly update still saves when an address lookup fails or an identifier is missing. ([#362](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/362))
- Action badges in the contractor page header no longer shrink or get squeezed. ([#360](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/360))
- Verified start and end dates now show in project scopes and schedules. Verifying a timeline no longer overwrites the original dates, and publish wizard week numbers match the renovation updates. ([#355](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/355))
- Creating or updating a brokerage no longer fails when its logo can't be processed, such as some AVIF files. The original logo is kept. ([#357](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/357))
- The brokerage logo picker accepts PNG, JPG and WebP, and shows an error message for other files instead of crashing. ([#357](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/357))
- Estimate line items and subtotals show TBD whenever any add-on is still TBD, in admin and on the dashboard. Price differences no longer treat partly TBD items as fully priced. ([#327](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/327))
- Creating a referral for a contact with no last name now shows a clear error pointing you to update the contact in HubSpot, instead of silently doing nothing. ([#364](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/364))
- Closing the review preview now asks you to confirm discarding unsaved changes, and no longer leaves a stale unsaved-changes warning behind. ([#324](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/324))
- The Flip360 section is always visible to admins in reports. ([#351](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/351))
- Completing a signed estimate no longer creates duplicate HIC project scopes. ([#335](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/335))

### Platform

**Improved**

- **Safer, quieter error monitoring:** Sentry now reports only from production. Passwords, tokens and other sensitive data are redacted from logs, and debug and trace logs are left out to cut noise. ([#353](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/353), [#325](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/325))
- **Page view tracking and cleaner review app checks:** Mixpanel now records page views on in-app navigation, not just full page loads. Pull requests no longer show a false "had a problem deploying" error from Heroku review apps. ([#302](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/302), [#330](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/330))

</Update>
