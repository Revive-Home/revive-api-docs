<Update label="October 8, 2026">

### A new profile and sidebar to manage your account

Edit your details, photo, and verified phone number in a redesigned profile, with a refreshed sidebar that collapses to save space. You can also review the latest features you've missed since your last login from your profile.

[Open your profile →](https://dashboard.revive.realestate/profile)

### Show off progress at the halfway mark

Once a project reaches 50% complete and has a good photo from a published weekly update, Revive offers a 50% completion marketing image. Share it to give clients a timely look at your work in progress.

_For agent accounts._

[Open Marketing Center →](https://dashboard.revive.realestate/marketing-center)
