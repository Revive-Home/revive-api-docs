<Update label="August 27, 2026" description="admin-v3.15.0, dashboard-v2.13.0, api-v3.12.0" tags={["dashboard","admin","api","platform"]}>

**Highlights:** The web dashboard gets a full construction project view with progress, photos, team and contract tabs. Staff get AI review of equity documents, a nearest contractors card, bulk RMA actions, and an Active Constructions map.

### Dashboard

**New**

- **Project dashboard for renovations:** Each project now has a web dashboard with summary, log, scope, photos, assets, team, marketing and contract views. Everything about a renovation is in one place. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))
- **Progress tracking and weekly updates:** Projects show progress, timelines, weekly updates and check-ins. Photo galleries support shared photo imports, and you can search and download documents. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))
- **Team invitations, comments and feedback:** You can invite people to a project and edit their roles. Team members can leave comments and feedback, and engagement notifications keep everyone in the loop. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))

**Improved**

- **Update materials pauses when image quota is low:** The Update materials button is disabled with a "Temporarily unavailable" tooltip when the image service has too little quota left. This protects agents' templates from being deleted and not rebuilt. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- **New link for agent lead-capture pages:** Lead-capture pages now live at /reviveai/, separate from Revive AI reports. QR codes, copied links and menus use the new address, and old /revive-ai/ links redirect so printed QR codes keep working. ([#254](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/254))

**Fixed**

- Update materials no longer deletes an agent's marketing templates when the image service can't regenerate them. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- One failed template no longer leaves an agent with no marketing templates, because the rest of the kit is still created. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- Photos that can't be converted from HEIC now upload as the original file instead of failing. ([#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))
- Meetings with no organizer information no longer cause errors. ([#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))
- Uploaded images with accents or special characters in the file name now save and download reliably. ([#372](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/372))

### Admin

**New**

- **Renovation activity and document controls:** Staff can manage renovation activities and agreement statuses, set required documents, and control project timelines. Multi-file uploads are supported. ([#229](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/229))
- **Nearest contractors card on opportunities:** Opportunity overviews list nearby contractors with names, tap-to-call phone numbers and distances, plus a map. Five show by default, up to 20 are available. Contractors without stored coordinates are located automatically. ([#414](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/414))
- **Bulk status updates on the RMA list:** Select rows and mark them Paid, Hold or Not Approved, with a confirmation and notes. Locked or completed items can't be changed by accident. You can also export the selected rows to CSV. ([#410](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/410))
- **RMA tabs with their own columns and filters:** The RMA list has configurable tabs, each with its own columns, sorting, filters and date range. It shows pipeline stage and payment dates, and remembers your tab, filters and page. ([#410](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/410))
- **Partial payments for outside vendor invoices:** Outside vendor payments are grouped by invoice, with payment progress, balances and status. You can start a payment request from an invoice's remaining balance with details prefilled. Partially paid invoices now have their own status. ([#411](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/411))
- **Contract previews in the RMA list:** Eligible contracts can be previewed from the RMA list. Finalized contracts download as a preview, and drafts open for editing. Loading and error messages are shown. ([#380](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/380))
- **MLS photo import in Revive AI intake:** Import photos from a listing URL into the photo and document uploads. Existing, new and MLS photos are managed together, and photo analysis shows progress while uploads finish. ([#378](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/378))

**Improved**

- **Marketing center buttons pause when quota is low:** Add to all agents and Add to one agent are disabled with a tooltip when image quota is low. Remove from all agents stays available. ([#400](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/400))
- **RMA progress in finance views:** Finance views show RMA progress, statuses, dates and final indicators, and work on smaller screens. ([#411](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/411))
- **Weekly updates check the project end date:** If a project's end date has passed, publishing a weekly update requires a future replacement date. Clearer warnings and date guidance appear while publishing, and on-schedule updates no longer show date ranges. ([#395](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/395))
- **More contract details in RMA info:** RMA information now shows contract ID, deal type, pipeline stage and requested amount. The contract breakdown section is removed. ([#380](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/380))

**Fixed**

- RMA date filters, groupings, totals and item counts now respect time zones, so they match the dates you pick. ([#410](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/410))
- Payment requests are checked against the invoice balance, so duplicate or excessive requests are blocked. ([#411](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/411))
- Delayed-project calculations stay correct when weekly updates are approved, unpublished or deleted. ([#395](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/395))
- Project schedule details refresh after a weekly update is published or removed. ([#395](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/395))
- Photos that fail to download now show a warning instead of being silently skipped, and an import with no successful downloads is blocked. ([#378](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/378))

### API

**New**

- **AI review of equity documents:** Uploaded listing agreements, mortgage statements, title reports, trust documents and reconveyances are reviewed automatically. Findings flag missing, outdated, inconsistent or illegible information. Staff can rerun a review and get results in Slack. ([#398](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/398))
- **Equity verification notes:** The system now drafts equity verification notes with financial calculations and review findings, and posts them automatically. Underwriting completion also captures vesting and debt information. ([#398](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/398))
- **Active Constructions map:** Properties in construction in the Production and Finance pipelines appear as pins on the map. Pins are added, updated and removed daily at midnight California time, and disappear at Check Received. ([#391](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/391))

**Improved**

- **More detail on Active Constructions pins:** Pins show the deal type, deal stage and renovation amount, with a button to open the HubSpot deal. Pin colors now reflect the deal type. ([#405](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/405))
- **Contractor Services Agreement document ID renamed:** The document's internal identifier is now contractor_services_agreement. The title stays "Contractor Services Agreement", and existing contractor records keep working. ([#416](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/416))

**Fixed**

- Completed contract, estimate and RMA PDFs from PandaDoc download more reliably, with automatic retries and clearer errors when a download fails. ([#370](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/370))
- Contracts that are already completed are no longer processed twice, and partial signer progress is handled correctly. ([#370](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/370))

### Platform

**Improved**

- **Preview deployments use the right API:** Preview builds now point at their pull request's own review API, and a new check fails loudly if they don't. This avoids previews that quietly test against staging. ([#394](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/394))
- **Firestore security rules in version control:** Firestore and Storage rules are now tracked in code. Staging deploys automatically, and production is a manual step. Writes now require sign-in. ([#377](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/377))
- **Cleaner error reporting:** Browser extensions no longer flood error reports, and unhandled promise rejections are handled better. Failed report associations keep more error detail. ([#393](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/393))

</Update>

<Update label="August 13, 2026" description="admin-v3.14.0, dashboard-v2.12.0, api-v3.11.0" tags={["dashboard","admin","api","platform"]}>

**Highlights:** Admin adds outside vendor payment requests in Finance and a contract breakdown on RMA pages. Weekly updates now accept documents and get AI-written summaries in the background.

### Dashboard

**Improved**

- **Tidier Revive AI report header:** Agents get a single More menu for Customize, View, Activity and Delete. Homeowners get a Share menu with Copy link and Download PDF. Both views have a button to add or view home photos. ([#341](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/341))
- **Ad click tracking at signup:** Ad click IDs from signup links are saved for up to 90 days and added to the user's HubSpot contact. This credits ads for people who sign up in the app. Failures never block signup. ([#343](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/343))
- **Loading message after picking an address:** After you pick an address, a loading message shows while the property loads. The old suggestion row no longer reappears and invites a second click. ([#347](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/347))
- **Feedback while additional photos process:** A "Processing your photos" screen appears right after you choose photos. Non-HEIC photos load faster, and one unreadable file no longer freezes the upload. ([#359](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/359))
- **Loading state in the comparables photo gallery:** The comparables window shows "Loading photos…" while photos are fetched, instead of flashing "No photos available". Switching comparables clears the previous selection. ([#346](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/346))

**Fixed**

- Project timelines, scopes, progress reports and weekly updates show verified start and end dates when available, instead of the original dates. ([#355](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/355))
- Saving a profile no longer errors from a brokerage mismatch, and a brokerage is only re-associated when you pick a new one. ([#290](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/290))

### Admin

**New**

- **Outside vendor payments:** Request payments to outside vendors with ACH or payment links, W-9 confirmation and notes. Finance sees these alongside RMAs in grouped check-run views, with CSV export, rush payment dates and vendor management. ([#271](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/271))
- **Contract breakdown on RMA pages:** RMA detail pages show the contract, itemized amounts, payments collected and the remaining balance. The breakdown refreshes when key RMA details change. ([#365](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/365))
- **Execution and rush payment dates for RMAs:** The RMA dashboard has sortable Execution Date and Rush Payment Date columns, and both dates are in CSV exports. Missing dates show as a dash. ([#334](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/334))
- **Documents in weekly updates:** Upload documents next to photos in weekly updates. Previews show file details and PDF page counts. Files over 25 MB or unsupported types are rejected with a clear message. Documents appear in review and in the dashboard. ([#333](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/333))
- **AI summaries for contractor weekly updates:** Contractor weekly updates now get an AI-written summary in the background, so submitting isn't delayed. You can regenerate a summary, and updates still save if summary generation fails. ([#362](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/362))
- **Subcontractor tab in Contractors:** Contractors has a separate Subcontractor tab. Subcontractors show up in contractor search. Their pages hide project stats and deal details and give more room to contact information. ([#360](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/360))
- **Cloud photo folder links on deals:** Link a Dropbox, Google Drive, Google Photos, OneDrive or iCloud folder to a deal. The link shows on the deal photo thumbnail and opens in a new tab. ([#354](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/354))
- **Changes highlighted in estimate version history:** Version history marks added, edited, recalculated and removed items, with subtotal differences and AI-written tooltips. It also shows the total estimate for each version. ([#316](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/316))

**Improved**

- **Publish wizard for weekly updates:** Create, preview and publish weekly updates through the publish wizard. It opens in a new tab and the update list refreshes when you return. The old editing and creation flows are gone. ([#363](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/363))
- **Safer brokerage logo uploads:** The brokerage logo picker accepts PNG, JPG and WebP, and shows a message when a file is rejected. Creating or updating a brokerage now succeeds even if a logo can't be processed. ([#357](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/357))

**Fixed**

- An estimate line item now shows TBD whenever any of its add-ons is TBD, instead of looking fully priced. ([#327](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/327))
- Creating a referral for a contact with missing details now shows a clear error that points to the contact record, instead of failing silently. ([#364](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/364))
- Closing the preview in review mode asks you to confirm before discarding unsaved changes, and clears the unsaved-changes flag when you leave. ([#324](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/324))
- The 360 report section is now visible to admins. ([#351](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/351))
- Declining a rush payment or RMA rush request now clears the payment date that was recorded for it. ([#334](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/334))

### API

**Improved**

- **Notes in Slack payment notifications:** Payment and RMA Slack notifications now include the submitted note when there is one. Special characters in notes no longer break the message. ([#315](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/315))
- **Connection to the Revive property service:** The API can now reach the Revive property service through a secure server-side connection. It keeps working when the service isn't configured. There are no visible changes yet. ([#314](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/314))

**Fixed**

- Completing a signed estimate no longer creates duplicate HIC project scopes. ([#335](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/335))

### Platform

**Improved**

- **Cleaner monitoring and logs:** Sensitive data such as passwords and tokens is redacted from logs. Error monitoring runs only in production, and debug logs are left out of it. ([#353](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/353), [#325](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/325))
- **Review apps and page-view tracking:** Pull request review apps no longer show false failed-deploy errors. Mixpanel now records page views on in-app navigation across all apps. ([#330](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/330), [#302](https://github.com/Revive-Home/revive-apps/pull/302))

</Update>
